Compressed Quantum Dynamics and Emergent Tensor-Network Geometry in Operator Systems

Author: Jeet Mozumdar

PDF: Download PDF

DOI (Zenodo): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20687402

Abstract

We study compressed Heisenberg-picture dynamics defined by a unital completely positive compression map $\mathbb E_{\mathcal M}$ whose image is an operator system $\mathcal A_{\mathcal M}$ labeled by a tensor-network geometry $(G,D)$. For physical evolutions $\Lambda_k$, the manifold-induced channels $\Phi_{\mathcal M}^{(k)}=\mathbb E_{\mathcal M}\circ\Lambda_k$ form a class that is exactly closed under composition, and we bound the difference between step-wise compression and compression applied once at the end of the evolution. The bound is stated pointwise on propagated observables and is informative only when these remain close to $\mathcal A_{\mathcal M}$.

Tensor-network geometry is connected to this structure through an explicit representability hypothesis: every state on $\mathcal A_{\mathcal M}$ lies within $\eta_D$ of the restriction of a tensor-network state with graph $G$ and bond dimension at most $D$. Under this hypothesis the standard Schmidt-rank argument gives a graph-cut mutual-information bound for the representatives and a geometric capacity functional $2\log D\,|\partial_G|$. These geometric statements are conditional on the hypothesis: the geometry enters through the hypothesis and the admissibility condition on $\mathbb E_{\mathcal M}$, and is not derived from the operator-algebraic layer. We do not construct a nontrivial admissible compression map; constructing one and deriving representability are left as open problems.

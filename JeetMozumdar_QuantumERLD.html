Entanglement Reachability and Structural Confinement Under Local Quantum Dynamics: An Axiomatic Approach With Illustrative Realizations

Author: Jeet Mozumdar

PDF: Download PDF

DOI (Zenodo): https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21846211

ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0005-4371-2416

Abstract

We study entanglement reachability in quantum many-body systems evolving under local dynamics. We formulate an axiomatic setting (locality, finite-dimensional memory, and representability constraints expressed through operator subsystems) and define reachable, forbidden and kinematically admissible entanglement structures on an induced structural space. We show that, once confinement constants are defined from the reachable set, structural confinement holds by construction, and we isolate the non-definitional input: a representability-invariance estimate (a restatement of the representability axiom) and two explicit hypotheses, one asserting the existence of an unreachable structure and one asserting that a family of structural observables detects all unreachable structures. We instantiate the setting in three realizations (GHZ coherence, an abstract operator-subsystem model adapted to matrix product operators, and protected logical information), in each of which the exclusion of a kinematically admissible structure follows from the representability axiom for a chosen projection. All results are conditional on the stated axioms and hypotheses.
